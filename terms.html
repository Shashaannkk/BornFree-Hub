Loyalty Governance

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 2026 · BONFREE-HUB Loyalty Club

1. Membership Eligibility

BONFREE-HUB Loyalty Club membership is open to customers aged 18 and above with one unique verified email account per person. Membership passes are personal and non-transferable.

2. Earning Stamps & Verification

Under the Classic Stamps default programme, 1 stamp is issued for each ₹1,000 of qualifying in-store purchase verified by authorized BONFREE-HUB staff against a unique store receipt number. Duplicate receipt submissions are rejected automatically.

3. Validity, Rewards & Redemptions

Unless stated otherwise on a promotional campaign, earned stamps remain valid for 12 months and unlocked reward vouchers remain valid for 6 months from issuance. Each voucher code is single-use, non-cash-redeemable, and verified at store checkout.

4. Fair Use & Support

BONFREE-HUB reserves the right to audit duplicate transactions, revoke improperly obtained stamps upon order cancellation or return, or suspend accounts involved in fraud. Questions may be sent to care@bonfree-hub.in.
