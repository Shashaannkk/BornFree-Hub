Member Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · BONFREE-HUB Retail & Loyalty

1. Information We Collect

BONFREE-HUB collects only the member details necessary to operate your digital loyalty account: your full name, email address, mobile number, birth month (optional for birthday rewards), qualifying in-store purchase references, stamp ledger history, and voluntary feedback survey responses.

2. Opaque Membership QR Security

Your digital loyalty pass QR code encodes solely a non-sensitive opaque membership identifier (for example, BFQR-…). It never embeds your email, phone number, password, or authorization tokens, and scanning alone cannot award stamps or redeem rewards without verified store-staff authentication and a qualifying receipt.

3. Storage, Access Control & Zero Sale of Data

Member accounts are stored in PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS) so members may access only their own profile, stamp cards, and vouchers. BONFREE-HUB never sells or rents customer personal information to third-party advertisers.

4. Member Rights & Contact

You may update your profile details or communication preferences inside the Customer Dashboard at any time. To request account export or deletion, write to care@bonfree-hub.in or visit our Contact Page.
